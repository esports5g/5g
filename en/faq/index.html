FAQ

Can I make a reservation?

If you have an account and have completed identity verification, you can make a paid reservation through the member portal. The same rates apply as for a regular PC session.

Do you lend game accounts?

Please prepare your own accounts.

Which games are supported?

Our PCs meet the recommended specs for most games, but we do not guarantee operation for each title. Titles not pre‑installed can be installed by users.

Payments

We accept cash, credit cards, transit IC cards, iD, QUICPay, PayPay, WeChat Pay, and Alipay.

Can I bring my own peripherals?

Yes. You can bring your own mouse, keyboard, or other peripherals. Please connect them and check that they work with the PC yourself. Compatibility with every device cannot be guaranteed.

Can I download and install my own games?

Yes. You can download and install games yourself. When the PC shuts down, it resets to its original state and clears all locally saved data, including downloaded games and settings.

Minor policy

Under 18: not allowed 22:00–05:00.

ID required

An ID is required for first‑time membership registration. Photos are not accepted; please bring the original.
