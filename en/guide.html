GUIDE

Starting and ending your session

  1. Choose any available seat. Points are deducted at different rates depending on your seat type and pricing plan, such as Regular or High-spec.
  2. Press the white power button on top of the PC to start it.
  3. After identity verification, enter your login details on the PC. Existing members use their Account ID. New members can use their email address or Account ID. Enter your password and select “Start session” (利用開始).
  4. Your balance is deducted according to your time and pricing plan. Any unexpired balance can be used on future visits. Ask the front desk to top up.
  5. You are responsible for monitoring your session time and balance. The café accepts no liability for issues concerning session time or balance.
  6. If you step away, use “Away lock” (離席ロック). Set a 4- to 6-digit PIN and use the same PIN to unlock the PC when you return. Charges continue while you are away.
  7. Your session may end automatically if your balance is insufficient. Check your balance and remaining time to avoid interruptions.

Always end your session before leaving.

“End session” (利用を終了) → “Leave seat” (退席する)

Save your files and sign out of personal accounts, including game accounts, first.

Follow the steps above in the PC session panel and confirm that the end-of-session screen appears.

The PC turns off automatically when your session ends. Charges will continue if you do not complete these steps.

Do not switch off the PC directly.

To open the panel, double-click the NetCafe icon at the bottom right of the screen.
